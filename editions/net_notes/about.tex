{\booktitlefont\scshape The New English Translation}\par
The NET Bible (New English Translation) is a modern English translation prepared
by Biblical Studies Press with a team of biblical scholars. It is distinguished
by more than sixty thousand translators' notes explaining textual and
interpretive decisions, and was among the first major translations released
freely on the internet for study.
