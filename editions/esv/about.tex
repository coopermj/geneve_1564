{\booktitlefont\scshape The English Standard Version}\par
The English Standard Version (2001) is an ``essentially literal'' translation in
the Tyndale--King~James stream, a revision of the 1971 Revised Standard Version.
It was prepared by a Translation Oversight Committee and a team of more than one
hundred scholars, seeking word-for-word precision together with literary
dignity and readability for public worship and study.
