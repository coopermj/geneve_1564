{\booktitlefont\scshape The text and its annotations}\par
This edition sets the modern NET Bible text beneath the marginal annotations of
the Geneva Study Bible --- the notes of the English Reformation Bible first
printed at Geneva in 1560 and revised through 1599. The NET (New English
Translation) was prepared by Biblical Studies Press with a team of biblical
scholars and is known for its tens of thousands of translators' notes; the
Geneva notes represent the study apparatus that shaped English-speaking
Protestantism for a century.
